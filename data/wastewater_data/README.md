##### Influenza A CDC Wastewater dataset: 

-	(WSAL) Wastewater Viral Activity Level: Recommendations:

-	Up to 2.7 – Very Low
-	Greater than 2.7 and up to 6.2 – Low
-	Greater than 6.2 and up to 11.2 – Moderate
-	Greater than 11.2 and up to 17.6 – High
-	Greater than 17.6 – Very High
  As per the recommendation of the CDC Waste water dataset .
